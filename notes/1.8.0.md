Kind: feature

- **Leaves rebuilt caches out of the data history, as their owners declare.** A folder under `.kehikot/` that its module declares a cache rebuilt from elsewhere is no longer committed, even without a `.gitignore` of its own. A folder's own `.gitignore` still works as before.

## For agents

- The data repository's staging is `git add -A`, honouring every nested `.gitignore`, minus the folders declared `derived` in the `module.folders` service (protocol 2.85.0), read through `services.find` (`services:read`). History keeps no list of other modules' folders and owns no folder under `.kehikot/` itself, so it declares none.
