- **Both histories of a project, side by side.** History shows the project's own git history and the history of Kehikot's material in the project (`.kehikot/`, where Checklist, Notes, Learning, Journeys and others keep their data).
- **A history for Kehikot's material.** When the project ignores that folder or has no repository, History can give it its own, so changes to your notes, checklists and journeys can be looked back on and undone.
- **Looks before it acts.** If the project's repository already keeps that folder, History says so and makes no second one, and it makes nothing at all until you press.
