- **Search the history with ⌘F.** Press ⌘F in History to open Kehikot's find bar. Every match in the commits and changes on screen is highlighted and counted ("3 of 17"), and the list scrolls to the current one. Enter and ⇧Enter move between matches, and Escape closes the bar.
- **Opens where you left it.** Each History container remembers how far down you had scrolled, and comes back there when the kehikko is opened again.
- **No "Loading…" flash.** Kehikot keeps its cover over the container until the history is read and drawn, then shows it whole.
- **A page that fails says so.** If History ever fails to draw, Kehikot is told and shows what went wrong instead of an empty container.
